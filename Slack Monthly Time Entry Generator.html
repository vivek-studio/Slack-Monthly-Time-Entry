Window = latest − earliest message in the working day (not the sum of gaps). Messages 12:00–06:30 AM (inclusive) count toward the previous day. Replies are deduplicated by timestamp + user. Days with no messages are simply absent (“No Slack Activity”), never zero-hour entries. Durations are rounded to whole minutes per day; totals sum those rounded values so exports match the screen.
